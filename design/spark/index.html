Spark

A director's viewfinder framing a four-point spark: the moment a film clicks. The frame stands for cinema; the spark stands for discovery and Genie's recommendations.

Primary symbol

The primary symbol is the duotone version (ink or white frame, amber spark). Use the single-color versions when only one ink is available.

Compact symbol

The spark on its own is for spaces under about 20 px: favicons, the notification icon, tab badges. At that size the frame turns to noise, but the spark stays crisp.

Android launcher

The adaptive icon is an amber symbol on an ink background, inside the 66 dp safe zone of a 108 dp canvas. White on amber was dropped because its contrast is too low. Android 13+ themed icons use the monochrome layer.
